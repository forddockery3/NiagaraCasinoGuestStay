What the app does

Enjoy a clear and convenient mobile experience that brings essential hotel services together in one place.

Guest Requests

Submit requests for room amenities, housekeeping, food and beverage service, laundry and garment care, maintenance support, transportation, concierge assistance, and special occasions. Enter your room number, describe what you need, and follow the current status of your request.

Concierge Chat

Ask about dining, taxis, spa, Wi‑Fi, and other guest services and get quick answers during your stay.

Event Booking

Discover upcoming events across the hotel complex, view event descriptions, dates, times, and current availability, and reserve places for yourself and your guests.

In-Room Dining

Browse the menu by category, explore dish descriptions, ingredients, prices, and preparation times, add meals to your cart, and place an order for delivery to your room.

Parking

Use the interactive parking map to explore available spaces across three parking floors. Select a space, choose your reservation dates and times, and manage active, upcoming, and previous parking reservations.

Hotel Blog

Explore useful articles about services, dining, events, facilities, and recommendations. Save interesting articles for later or share them using the native sharing options.

Guest QR Code

A dynamic guest QR code is available throughout the app for quick presentation at reception without displaying personal guest information.
