Support

Common questions about Niagara Casino Guest Stay.

What is Niagara Casino Guest Stay?

A convenient hotel companion that helps guests manage essential services throughout their stay: guest requests, concierge chat, event reservations, in-room dining, parking, hotel content, and a dynamic guest QR code.

Can I play casino games or wager money in the app?

No. Niagara Casino Guest Stay is only for hotel guest services during a stay. It does not include slots, table games, sports betting, deposits, withdrawals, or any real-money gaming.

Do I need to sign up or log in to use it?

No account is required to use the main guest features. Support contact is available by email if you need help.

How do I create a guest request?

Open Guest Requests and choose a category: room amenities, housekeeping, food and beverage, laundry and garment care, maintenance, transportation, concierge assistance, or special occasions. Enter your room number, describe what you need, and follow the current status of your request.

How does Concierge chat work?

Use Concierge chat to ask about dining, taxis, spa, Wi‑Fi, and other guest services and get quick answers during your stay.

How do event reservations work?

Discover upcoming events across the hotel complex, view descriptions, dates, times, and current availability, then reserve places for yourself and your guests.

How do I order in-room dining?

Browse the in-room dining menu by category, explore dish descriptions, ingredients, prices, and preparation times, add meals to your cart, and place an order for delivery to your room.

How does parking reservation work?

Use the interactive parking map to explore available spaces across three parking floors. Select a space, choose your reservation dates and times, and manage active, upcoming, and previous parking reservations.

How does the hotel blog work?

Explore useful articles about services, dining, events, facilities, and recommendations. Save interesting articles for later or share them using the native sharing options.

What is the guest QR code?

A dynamic guest QR code is available throughout the app for quick presentation at reception without displaying personal guest information.
