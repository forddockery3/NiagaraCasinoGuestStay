About the app

Niagara Casino Guest Stay is a hotel guest service iPhone application.

Purpose

Niagara Casino Guest Stay is a convenient hotel companion designed to help guests manage essential services and activities throughout their stay. The application and its supporting website are developed by PATTERN BARBER SHOP LIMITED, with MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. associated with the Niagara Casino brand.

Request room assistance, chat with concierge, reserve places at hotel events, order meals to your room, manage parking reservations, explore useful hotel content, and present a dynamic guest QR code — all from one application.

Guest Requests

Room amenities, housekeeping, food and beverage, laundry and garment care, maintenance, transportation, concierge assistance, and special occasions. Enter your room number, describe what you need, and follow request status.

Concierge chat

Ask about dining, taxis, spa, Wi‑Fi, and other guest services and get quick answers during your stay.

Events & dining

View event descriptions, dates, times, and availability, then reserve places. Browse the dining menu by category and order delivery to your room.

Parking & blog

Interactive map across three parking floors with active, upcoming, and previous reservations. Hotel articles with save and native sharing.

Guest QR code

A dynamic guest QR code for quick presentation at reception without displaying personal guest information.

Platform

Designed for iPhone guests during a stay.
